Comparative literature across languages, cultures and worlds.

I am a scholar of comparative and world literature, with particular interests in translation, cultural memory, environmental humanities and the applied dimensions of literary research.

Research

Comparative research across texts, territories and cultural systems.

My research examines literary circulation, translation, world literature, cultural memory and the relationship between literature and place.

I am particularly interested in comparative methods that connect textual analysis with cartography, digital approaches and public-facing forms of humanities research.
